Who I Am

I am someone who is still learning about myself and becoming more comfortable with who I am. I am learning to understand and accept myself more each day.

As I continue growing, I am learning that being true to myself is important. I also learned that having people who understand and support me can make a big difference.

What I Believe

I believe that everyone deserves respect and the freedom to become the person they want to be. I want to continue becoming a better person while staying true to myself.
